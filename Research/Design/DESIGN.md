## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a focused, high-clarity environment engineered specifically for data-dense B2B marketing audits, campaign performance reviews, and creative quality assurance. Operating at the intersection of modern corporate precision and energetic analytics, it projects institutional confidence, meticulous governance, and operational velocity.

### Visual Aesthetic & Movement
Drawing inspiration from contemporary SaaS tools and corporate analytical systems, the aesthetic balances functional utility with vibrant, goal-oriented accents. The interface relies on crisp structured containment, sharp semantic contrast, and modular card units. Layouts emphasize scan-friendly tables, segmented workflow switchers, and glanceable status badges that turn complex multi-channel creative pipelines into immediate, actionable insights.

### Emotional Resonance
- **Precision & Reliability:** Unwavering baseline structure and dependable alignment make dense marketing datasets feel digestible and systematic.
- **Vigilance & Velocity:** Strategic high-contrast alerts highlight compliance bottlenecks, budget variances, and creative inconsistencies before campaigns launch.
- **Empowerment:** Clean surfaces, subdued divider lines, and purposeful color coding ensure marketing directors and auditors maintain total oversight across distributed collateral.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from thematic audit pillars, balancing structural anchors with distinct functional roles across data visualizations and operational workflows.

### Primary Role: Innovation & Primary Action (`#3b66d4`)
The royal electric blue serves as the foundational interactive driver. It anchors primary CTAs, active workflow navigation states, focused table selections, and critical KPI highlights. It commands immediate focus without introducing visual fatigue.

### Secondary Role: Critical Audit Alerts & Overviews (`#e05869`)
A vivid coral crimson reserved for high-priority audit warnings, non-compliant creative assets, system escalations, and critical review metrics. It signals urgency while remaining visually harmonious with the core palette.

### Tertiary Role: Review Lifecycle & Attention (`#e78e63`)
A warm terracotta tone allocated to items in transition—pending approval gates, intermediate compliance scores, and sustainability or tracking audits that require attention before completion.

### Supporting Tone: Muted Badges & Subdued Surfaces (`#f59fa0`)
A soft salmon blush applied at low opacities (`8%` to `16%`) for subtle tag backgrounds, low-contrast warning badges, and accent container fills.

### Structural Base: Navy Slate (`#1f2a44`) & Neutral System
- **Slate Navy (`#1f2a44`):** Anchors high-contrast primary text, main titles, dark headers, and dense sidebar hierarchies.
- **Border Neutral (`#e2e8f0`):** Provides sharp, structural separation for tables, input perimeters, and card containers.
- **Canvas (`#f8fafc` to `#ffffff`):** Multi-tier white and cool gray backgrounds that elevate high-density data tables and nested cards.

## Typography

Typography is standardized on Inter across all hierarchical roles. This ensures optical balance, exceptional horizontal tabular figure alignment, and uncompromised legibility in micro-data layouts.

### Hierarchy & Utilization
- **Displays & Headlines:** Set with tighter tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.025em`) and semi-bold/bold weights to anchor section boundaries and top-level dashboard metrics.
- **Body:** Calibrated at 14px (`body-md`) for standard data consumption and audit notes, keeping vertical line rhythm steady across expansive multi-column tables.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** Rendered in medium to semi-bold weights with gentle positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to ensure status tags, column metadata, and batch chips remain legible at 11px and 12px.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements a structured 12-column fluid grid paired with an 8px base rhythm (with 4px half-step sub-increments). Layouts prioritize functional data density without compromising structural breathing room.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adapters
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) screen margins. Sidebars occupy a fixed 260px container, allowing audit tables and analytics panels to stretch flexibly.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column configuration with 16px (`1rem`) gutters. Metric summary widgets collapse from 4-across to 2x2 grids. The left navigation panel transitions into an off-canvas drawer or icon rail.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column flow with 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Tables convert to vertical card stacks, and secondary metric filters collapse behind a segmented bottom sheet.

### Spacing Rules
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for internal pill padding, table cell vertical balance, and inline label-icon pairs.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for standard container interior padding and list gaps.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) to `space-xl` (32px) for card-to-card module separations and dashboard section breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain clarity and support dense data tables, elevation relies primarily on low-contrast structural borders rather than heavy diffused shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Neutral background `#f8fafc`. No border or shadow. Hosts background workflows and structural page regions.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Data Panels):** Solid `#ffffff` background with a 1px border (`#e2e8f0`) and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(31, 42, 68, 0.05)`. Used for audit tables, metric widgets, and workflow groups.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Flyouts & Filter Dropdowns):** Solid `#ffffff` surface with a 1px border (`#cbd5e1`) and a directional shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 42, 68, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 42, 68, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Audit Drilldown Sheets):** Elevated layer accompanied by a translucent backdrop overlay (`rgba(31, 42, 68, 0.40)`). Shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(31, 42, 68, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced `roundedness: 2` geometry. This creates an 8px (`0.5rem`) default radius that keeps the UI modern and structured without appearing overly play-oriented or aggressively sharp.

### Boundary Tokens
- **Base (8px / 0.5rem):** Applied to buttons, inputs, segmented control chips, table hover rows, and alert notifications.
- **Large (16px / 1rem):** Applied to audit summary cards, workflow metric panels, and elevated modals.
- **Pill / Circular (Full):** Reserved exclusively for micro-status badges, audit counters, user avatars, and circular progress indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#3b66d4`, foreground `#ffffff`, border radius `0.5rem` (8px), height 38px (compact) or 44px (default). On hover, background shifts to `#3154b3`. Focus ring: 2px solid `#3b66d4` with 2px offset.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background, 1px border `#cbd5e1`, foreground `#1f2a44`. On hover, background `#f1f5f9`.
- **Destructive / Urgent Action:** Background `#e05869`, foreground `#ffffff`. On hover, background `#c94657`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Status badges feature an 11px semi-bold uppercase label, 4px horizontal padding, 2px vertical padding, and a full rounded radius.
- **Approved / Active:** Background `#ecfdf5`, text `#047857`, border `#a7f3d0`.
- **Flagged / Audit Failed:** Background `rgba(224, 88, 105, 0.12)`, text `#dc4c64`, border `rgba(224, 88, 105, 0.3)`.
- **Pending Review / In Progress:** Background `rgba(231, 142, 99, 0.14)`, text `#c4693e`, border `rgba(231, 142, 99, 0.35)`.
- **Subtle / Draft:** Background `rgba(245, 159, 160, 0.16)`, text `#1f2a44`, border `rgba(245, 159, 160, 0.4)`.

### Table Cards & Grid Views
- Structured as unified white panels enclosed by a 1px `#e2e8f0` border and 16px corner radius.
- Table headers utilize `#f8fafc` background, `#1f2a44` medium text, 12px uppercase labels, and 12px vertical padding.
- Row items utilize 14px text, hover states with subtle `#f8fafc` tint, and a 1px interior `#f1f5f9` bottom divider.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** 1px `#cbd5e1` border, 8px radius, white fill, 14px text color `#1f2a44`. Placeholder set to `#94a3b8`. Focus state introduces a crisp 1.5px `#3b66d4` outline without layout shift.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px square or circular bounds, 1.5px border `#94a3b8`. Selected state fills `#3b66d4` with a crisp white checkmark.

### Workflow Tabs & Navigation Rails
- Segmented tabs feature an enclosed `#f1f5f9` track with 8px radius. Active tab slides forward with a white surface, subtle 1px ambient shadow, and `#1f2a44` semi-bold text.
- Left-hand navigation rail employs dark slate navy (`#1f2a44`) headers, high-contrast active indicator strips rendered in `#3b66d4`, and clean icon-first audit category groupings.
